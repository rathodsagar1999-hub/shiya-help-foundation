WHO WE ARE

A helping hand can bring new hope.

SHIYA HELP FOUNDATION is a social-help initiative built around compassion, respect and practical support for people in need.

Our intention is to encourage community care and work towards assistance in areas such as food, education, health and essential needs. We believe support should be offered with dignity, fairness and transparency.

AREAS OF FOCUS

How we hope to help

These are our intended areas of support. Updates about completed activities will be published with accurate details.

🍚

Food & Essentials

Supporting families with essential groceries and daily necessities when assistance is available.

📚

Education Support

Encouraging learning by helping children access basic educational materials and resources.

🩺

Medical Assistance

Helping people connect with appropriate support for essential medical needs.

👕

Clothing & Care

Promoting the thoughtful sharing of useful clothing and other essential items.
